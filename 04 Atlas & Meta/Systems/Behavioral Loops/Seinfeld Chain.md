# Seinfeld Chain

## For future agent

> **Summary:** Atomic on Seinfeld's Don't Break The Chain — streak visualization → loss aversion. Status `synthesized` as of `2026-09-02`. Wire to `Tracker` reading `tracking.csv` — the chain *is* the reward.
> **Load:** Read `SOUL.md`.

## Definition

**Seinfeld Chain (timeless)** — `Do X daily → mark calendar → chain grows → don't break chain becomes reward.` Visual streak + loss aversion > motivation. The calendar mark *is* the variable-ish reward (chain length).

## Nuance

- ADHD needs *visible* streaks: Tracker chart, not memory. Vault already logs every edit to `04 Atlas & Meta/Logs/tracking.csv` — that's the chain data.
- WIP limit 5 prevents streak obsession: chain for *1* daily action (e.g., `1 sentence under ## Synthesis` or `1 Inbox capture`) not `ship essay daily` — that would break.
- Grace: chain breaks are data, not failure. `Parked` notes don't break chain — they *preserve* chain by reducing WIP.
- Best streak for this vault: `Capture → 00 Inbox daily` or `1 Pomodoro on active hyperfixation`.
