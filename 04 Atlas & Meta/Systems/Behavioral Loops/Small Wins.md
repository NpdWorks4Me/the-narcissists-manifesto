# Small Wins

## For future agent

> **Summary:** Atomic on Amabile's Progress Principle — small wins are the #1 dopamine predictor. Status `synthesized` as of `2026-09-02`. Every `resume_point.next_step` and kanban card must be a 15min win to guarantee reward.
> **Load:** Read `SOUL.md`.

## Definition

**Small Wins (Amabile & Kramer, 2011, timeless)** — `Tiny meaningful progress → disproportionate dopamine → momentum.` Progress on *meaningful* work is the strongest predictor of motivation, more than praise or money. Must be *win-shaped*: verb + done-state.

## Nuance

- ADHD overwhelm fix: shard `Draft step-by-step framework for hook implementation` → `Draft Hook Step 1: trigger checklist (5 bullets, 15m) 📅 today`
- Definition of Done is mandatory: `until [2 sentences under ## Synthesis]` or `until SPEC Hook section has thesis`. Without done, win never closes → no dopamine.
- Meaningful > trivial: `Map ONE variable reward to product hooks` (meaningful) beats `Organize tags` (trivial) — brain knows.
- Vault guarantee: every kanban card *is* a small win. Move `Ready → Doing → Done` is 3 wins, not 1. Triply rewarding.

- Win: `- [ ] If I open [[dopamine-loops]] then write 1 nuance sentence about ethical vs exploitative 📅 today`
- Not a win: `- [ ] Research hook model thoroughly` (no verb specificity, no done, no timebox)

## ADHD Self-Delegation

Delegate with win guaranteed: `VERB + FILE + DONE + TIMEBOX + DATE`. Example board: `Doing` has max 3 wins. Future-you picks top win — always closable in one pomodoro.
