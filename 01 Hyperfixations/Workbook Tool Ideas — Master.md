## 1) Worksheet Builder — Drag-drop DBT handout → your workbook

**One-line:** A templater where you drag any DBT/CBT handout (from your 14) onto a page, auto-formats it into your workbook style, and exports print-ready PDF.

**Tool idea (expanded):**
- **What it is:** Canvas + block editor: left pane = library of 14 source handouts (TF-CBT pages, DBT Assignment, Betweensessions sheets, Flinders 2-behavioural-activation). Drag a block (e.g., “Opposite Action” or “PLEASE”) onto your workbook page. Right pane = your house style (fonts, colors, header). One-click export to `A4 PDF` + `Obsidian markdown` for `04 Atlas & Meta/`.
- **Purpose:** Removes the bottleneck you hit when remixing handouts: manual copy-paste, reformatting, and rights-checking. Lets you test 3–5 handout flows in an afternoon and keep the ones that resonate for your own workbook, while preserving source citation (`as of 2026-09-02, betweensessions.com`) for OKM compliance.
- **Target audience:** (a) You — the workbook author — as primary builder; (b) later, readers who want to remix their own DBT/CBT home practice (therapists, peer supporters, highly self-directed clients). ADHD-friendly: drag-drop is a **Habit Loop** cue (visible block → small win in <2 min).
- **Why viable from intel:** `c6bf433d_DBT_Assignment_Workbook_F0220.pdf` (2.9MB) and `44eea194_TF-CBT-Workbook-Final.pdf` (881KB) already contain block-structured handouts. No new content needed for v1 — just a wrapper. Rights: Betweensessions/TF-CBT are free for personal/clinical use with attribution (verify before republishing).
- **Next step to test:** Drag the Flinders “Behavioural Activation — activity scheduling” page into a blank canvas, export, and compare readability vs original.

**Source intel:** Synthesized from `DBT Assignment Workbook + TF-CBT Workbook` (betweensessions.com + tfcbt.org)

## 2) Anxiety Reduction Tracker — daily 1-10 with behavioural activation tie-in

**One-line:** Daily 1-tap tracker (anxiety 1–10 + one BA activity) that graphs the link between *what you did* and *how you felt* — the core of the UARK workbook made tangible.

**Tool idea (expanded):**
- **What it is:** Mobile-first tracker (or `Tracker` plugin code block): each evening you log `Anxiety 1–10`, `Depression 1–10`, and *one* behavioural activation item (e.g., “walk 15 min”, “call friend”). Week view = line chart anxiety vs activity bars. Tap a dot → shows the handout exercise from `health.uark.edu` (e.g., “5-4-3-2-1 grounding”).
- **Purpose:** Makes the UARK `anxiety-depression reduction workbook` actionable without reading 20 pages. Turns an abstract loop (`behaviour → mood`) into a **Seinfeld Chain** (don’t break the chain) + **Small Wins** (1 activity = win). You get data for your workbook’s “does BA actually move the needle?” chapter.
- **Target audience:** (a) You — to dogfood the BA model while writing; (b) readers with anhedonia/anxiety who freeze on long worksheets — they need a 30-sec daily check, not a 12-page packet. Also useful as a therapist homework bridge.
- **Why viable:** `79af5699_caps-resources-anxiety-depression-reduction-workbook.pdf` (1.5MB, health.uark.edu) already has 1–10 scales and BA worksheets. No new clinical content — just a tracker shell. Complements your `loneliness` hyperfixation (BA is a loneliness antidote).
- **Next step:** Create a `Tracker` code block in `04 Atlas & Meta/Dashboards/` that reads `tracking.csv` — log 7 days manually, then decide if a hosted app is warranted.

**Source intel:** `health.uark.edu workbook` (caps-resources-anxiety-depression-reduction-workbook.pdf)

## 3) Life Story Plan — DBS Alliance LS workbook → guided timeline

**One-line:** Guided timeline where you (or a reader) plot life events, mark “turning points,” and auto-generates a one-page “Life Story Plan” handout from the DBS Alliance structure.

**Tool idea (expanded):**
- **What it is:** Timeline builder: prompts from `a349f530_LS_plan_workbook.pdf` (863KB, dbsalliance.org) — e.g., “Early years”, “First episode”, “Support system”, “Strengths”. User drags events onto a horizontal timeline, adds a 1-sentence “meaning” per event. Export = printable `LS Plan` handout matching the DBS Alliance layout, plus an Obsidian note with loneliness / narcissism-as-performance theme links.
- **Purpose:** The LS Plan is the most narrative, identity-level tool in your 14. It’s also the least “worksheet-y” and most workbook-worthy — it helps readers (and you) turn scattered confessional material (Raw Confessions, Mind UK) into a coherent story, which is the heart of *The Narcissist's Manifesto*. Turns **Zeigarnik** open loops (“my story is fragmented”) into a closed narrative.
- **Target audience:** (a) You — to structure your own manifesto arc; (b) readers doing autobiographical work (BPD/NPD-adjacent, loneliness, or anyone needing a “my story” scaffold). Also valuable for peer-support facilitators. Not a clinical intake — a reflective planning tool.
- **Why viable:** `a349f530_LS_plan_workbook.pdf` is explicitly designed as a fillable plan, with sections, examples, and rights for personal use. You can mirror its structure, cite `as of 2026-09-02, dbsalliance.org`, and add your own prompts derived from `loneliness` research.
- **Next step:** Rebuild one page of the LS workbook in Obsidian Canvas, test with your own 5 events, and see if the export reads as a standalone handout.

**Source intel:** `dbsalliance.org LS_plan` (LS_plan_workbook.pdf)
