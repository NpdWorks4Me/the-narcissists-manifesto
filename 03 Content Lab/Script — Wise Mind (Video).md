# Script — Wise Mind (Video)

One 6-minute YouTube episode, about 820 spoken words, plus one 35-40 second Short. Built from [[Article — Wise Mind]] and DBT Workbook Worksheet 9 Wise Mind and One-Mindfully.

No screen share. No live writing. No workbook HTML on camera. All visuals are static cards and Venn animation you can make once and reuse.

---

## Graphics note: make this your visual identity for the skill

Two overlapping circles on a dark background.

- Left circle: LOGIC in cool blue
- Right circle: EMOTION in warm orange
- Overlap: WISE MIND in white and gold

This Venn is your thumbnail, your intro graphic, your transitions, and your end card. One visual, learned once, remembered forever. It also becomes your thumbnail system for every episode in the Emotional Storm Survival series.

Additional cards you need, static PNG, 1920x1080:

- Card 1: Name the balance
- Card 2: Recall both sides
- Card 3: Relax and replay with kindness

## LONG-FORM SCRIPT, about 6 minutes

Read like you are explaining to a friend, not performing. Total spoken is about 820 words. At 135-145 words per minute plus graphic holds and one 5 second breath, this lands at 5:55 to 6:10.

| Time | Visual / Screen | Script |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00-0:25 | Face to camera. No title card yet. Captions on. | Your brain has two loud voices. One is a spreadsheet. One is a fire alarm. Most of your life, one of them wins. And that is the problem. Today I will show you the skill that lets both of them be right at the same time. It is called Wise Mind. |
| 0:25-1:20 | Venn appears. Circles labeled LOGIC and EMOTION. | DBT says you run on two gears. The reasonable mind is logic in charge. You can check facts, make a plan, wait for what you want, and sit with frustration without falling apart. You need it. The emotional mind is feelings in charge. You react fast, you feel a lot, you pick up threat fast. You need that one too, because feelings carry real data. Fear says this matters. Anger says a line was crossed. Hurt says something needs care. Neither gear is bad. When logic drives alone, life feels flat and cold. When emotion drives alone, life feels loud and rushed. Problems start when one gear drives alone. Think of it like eyes. Left eye sees facts. Right eye sees weight. You need both for depth. |
| 1:20-2:10 | Split screen graphic: The overthinker on left, The reactor on right. | Here is what it looks like stuck in one lane. The overthinker gathers facts and never picks. More lists. More pros and cons. No choice, because choice means feeling something. The reactor feels all of it at full volume and acts fast. Send the text. Quit the job. Say yes when you mean no. Then comes the cleanup and the apology. Both miss half the information. The overthinker has facts with no weight. The reactor has weight with no facts. Most of us lean one way. That lean is not a flaw. It is just your starting point. Today we practice the middle. |
| 2:10-2:50 | Point to the overlap. It lights up: WISE MIND. | Picture two circles. Left is logic. Right is emotion. The middle where they touch is Wise Mind. In Wise Mind you feel your true feelings and you still act with wisdom. You see gray, not just black and white. The workbook calls it gut intuition. That quiet knowing when you are not panicking and not freezing up to think. When your heart and your facts agree, that is it. You do not need to live there all day. You just need to visit more often. Let me show you how with one story. |
| 2:50-4:40 | Show Maya scenario text on screen. Then show Card 1, Card 2, Card 3, Card 4 in order. No workbook image. | Meet Maya. It is 9 pm. Her boss texts: We need to talk tomorrow. Just that. No detail. Her emotional mind fires first. Heart pounds. Thoughts race. I messed up. I am getting fired. I should reply now and fix it. Her reasonable mind tries to take over. It says: Stop. You have no facts. Talk could mean schedule. Talk could mean project update. Talk could mean feedback. No proof of firing. Both have a piece. Here are the four moves to put them together. One. Name the balance. Ask: which voice is louder right now? For Maya, emotion is at an 8 out of 10. Logic is at a 3. Just name it. No fixing yet. Two. Recall both sides. Maya reminds herself: logic helped last month when she made a rent plan step by step instead of panic. Emotion helped when her gut flagged a bad lease and she was right. Both have saved her before. Three. Relax the body and replay with kindness. Feet flat. Shoulders down. One slow breath in through the nose, out through the mouth. Now replay the text without shame. She was tired. Anyone would feel a jolt. Send yourself the same kindness you would send a friend. Four. Ask Wise Mind: what would a wise response look like here? Not the panicked answer. Not the cold answer. The overlap answer. For Maya it is: Feel the fear, do not send a midnight paragraph, write down two questions for tomorrow, sleep, then go in clear. Heart and facts in the same plan. |
| 4:40-5:20 | Card 5: phone face down next to plate. Simple B-roll or still photo. | Second half of the skill is One-Mindfully. It means one thing at a time, with full attention. When you eat, just eat. Maya picks one meal this week. Phone in another room. No show. Just taste, chew, pause. Pick your one too. Same meal. Same chair. Three slow breaths before bite one. After, she checks: how long did I stay with it? What pulled me away? What did I like? That is not a grade. That is data. If closing your eyes or slowing down feels hard, keep eyes open. Go shorter. The muscle still grows. |
| 5:20-6:00 | Face to camera. Venn behind you. End card fades in at last line. | Nobody lives in Wise Mind all day. Not monks. Not therapists. Not Maya. It is a muscle. One calm meal. One honest note. One time you feel the fire alarm and still choose. If you want practice, I linked a free DBT workbook with Worksheet 9 below. It has the four prompts and the weekly tracker. Try the Maya version first, then try yours with a small moment from your week. You can feel everything and still choose well. That is the skill. I will see you in the next one on riding the wave. |
| End card | Venn plus subscribe plus series name: Emotional Storm Survival. | Feel everything and still choose well. |

### Delivery notes

- No live fill. You never write on camera. The four cards carry the teaching.
- The Maya story replaces personal disclosure. Same rent and text themes from [[Article — Wise Mind]], but third person, so you do not have to share a real midnight text.
- The breath is 5 seconds, with voiceover. Demo it, do not describe it. Inhale on screen text, exhale on screen text, then keep talking.
- Record face to camera for start and end only. Middle can be voiceover plus cards. That makes editing and retakes simple.
- Captions on. Put each card title as on-screen text for muted viewers.
- Pinned comment idea: Which lane do you lean? Overthinker or reactor? Reply with 1 or 2.

### Reel notes

- First 3 seconds are the hook. Do not start with a greeting or a logo.
- The single visible breath at 18s is the emotional payoff. Keep it real.
- Caption every step as on-screen text. Most viewers watch muted.
