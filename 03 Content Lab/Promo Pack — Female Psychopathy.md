## Social post — Thread/X (7-post thread version)

1. We picture "psychopath" as a man, usually violent. There's a reason, and it's not just because it's true.
2. The research on psychopathy was built on male prisoners. The main tool, the PCL-R, was tested mostly on men.
3. So women got measured with a "man-shaped ruler." Some signs matter less for them, some matter more.
4. The 2021 data: psychopathy is roughly twice as common in men (~7.9%) as women (~2.9%). A real minority, still real.
5. When it shows up in women, it's often relational: charm, pity, twisting friendships. Not fists.
6. It's a health issue too, and it can echo across generations. Early help could break the cycle.
7. The goal isn't suspicion. It's clarity. Trust patterns, protect your peace, stay kind. [link]
