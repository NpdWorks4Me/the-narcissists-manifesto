## Hook

> Open with tension, question, or story. 1-2 lines.

- "Guilt is supposed to be the brakes. But for some people, it's the gas." — cold-open premise that everyone assumes feeling bad stops bad behavior, and then flips it.

## Thesis

> Single claim this piece will prove or explore.

- In personality-disordered individuals, the "inhibitory" emotions (shame, guilt, remorse) can paradoxically escalate abuse rather than stop it — so the reader should recognize the pattern and stop blaming themselves.
