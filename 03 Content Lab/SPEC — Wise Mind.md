# SPEC — Wise Mind: Where Logic and Emotion Finally Agree

## For future agent

> **Summary:** Section 3 content artifact for the channel's "Emotional Storm Survival" series. Explains DBT's three states of mind (reasonable, emotional, Wise Mind) plus One-Mindfully, at a 7th-grade level, in the house voice (clinical but lived-in; no em-dashes; plain register; never frame the emotional mind as a flaw). Package: SPEC + Article + long-form video script + reel script + Promo Pack. All skill content grounded in Betweensessions DBT Assignment Workbook Worksheet 9, not invented from memory.
> **Load:** When editing, read the draft article + script + the workbook worksheet section. The workbook is the source of truth for the skill steps. Keep the promise: "You can feel everything and still choose well."

## Hook

> Open with tension, question, or story. 1-2 lines.

- "Your brain has two loud voices. One is a spreadsheet. One is a fire alarm. Most of your life, one of them wins. DBT calls the place where both are true the Wise Mind."

## Thesis

- Wise Mind is the trainable overlap between logic and emotion: you can feel your feelings fully and still act with wisdom. Most people live stuck in one lane (overthinking or overreacting); the worksheet's four moves plus One-Mindfully practice pull you into the overlap.
