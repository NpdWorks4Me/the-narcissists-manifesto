# Internal Object Interaction (Introjection as Defense)

## Definition

Internal object interaction is a defensive process in which a person relates to a mental representation of another person instead of relating to the actual external person. In the source account, partners of individuals with borderline personality disorder develop an internal object—described as a “snapshot” of the borderline—that is reliable, caring, loving, empathic, and non-dangerous.
