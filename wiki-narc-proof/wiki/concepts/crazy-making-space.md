# Crazy-making space

The **crazy-making space** is a concept used in describing the psychopathic female: a space created by the female psychopath that is reminiscent of, or the equivalent of, the [[narcissist's shared fantasy]]. It is described as a vortex or whirlpool into which the psychopathic female draws ever-increasing concentric circles of people around her. Within this space she entertains, lures, baits, incorporates, digests, and assimilates those around her. ^[yt-uwaiowshp9i.md:9]

Most psychopaths are male; only a small minority — between one-quarter and one-third — are women. Female psychopaths tend to be factor-two or **secondary psychopaths**, marked by impulsivity, emotion dysregulation reminiscent of [[borderline personality disorder]], recklessness, and defiance. They may still experience overwhelming emotions, including empathy, shame, and guilt, yet they can nonetheless destroy lives. ^[yt-uwaiowshp9i.md:9]

## Characteristics

The abuse taking place in a crazy-making space is primarily relational rather than goal-oriented. The female psychopath abuses people in interpersonal relationships through [[smear campaign|smear campaigns]], cheating with friends, colleagues, or their spouses, and manipulating others by flirting or by begging for pity, casting men in rescuer or savior roles. She presents as the longed-for soulmate, twin flame, or other lost half, and may play roles ranging from the shy, vulnerable damsel in distress to the extroverted, histrionic "sex bomb." These roles are interchangeable acts, not reflections of true character.
