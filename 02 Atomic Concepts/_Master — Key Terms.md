## Behavioural Activation (BA)

**Definition:** A structured CBT strategy where you schedule and complete valued or mastery activities to counter the avoidance/withdrawal cycle of depression and anxiety. You track *what you did* and *how you felt* to see the link.

**Source:** `https://www.flinders.edu.au/.../2-behavioural-activation.pdf` (34MB PDF in `00 Inbox/652ac5a2_...pdf`) — core of Flinders BA handout.
**Use in workbook:** 1-page activity scheduling sheet + 7-day tracker (your Anxiety Reduction Tracker idea).

## TF-CBT (Trauma-Focused Cognitive Behavioral Therapy)

**Definition:** An 8-component model for youth with trauma/PTSD: **P**sychoeducation, **R**elaxation, **A**ffective modulation, **C**ognitive processing, **T**rauma narrative, **I**n vivo mastery, **C**onjoint parent sessions, **E**nhancing safety (PRACTICE). The workbook `Your Very Own TF-CBT Workbook` is its child-facing companion.

**Source:** `https://tfcbt.org/.../Your-Very-Own-TF-CBT-Workbook-Final.pdf` (881KB, `00 Inbox/44eea194_...pdf`).
**Use:** Adapt 1–2 PRACTICE handouts for your workbook’s “skills” section.

## DBT — Dialectical Behavior Therapy

**Definition:** A skills-based therapy balancing acceptance and change via 4 modules: **Mindfulness**, **Distress Tolerance**, **Emotion Regulation**, **Interpersonal Effectiveness**. Built for emotion dysregulation, widely reused in workbooks.

**Source:** `https://www.betweensessions.com/.../DBT_Assignment_Workbook_F0220.pdf` (2.9MB) + `https://archive.org/details/dbt-skills-handouts-and-worksheets` (Linehan handouts).
**Use:** Source for Worksheet Builder blocks (e.g., `Opposite Action`, `PLEASE`).

## Distress Tolerance

**Definition:** DBT module for surviving crises without making things worse. Key skills: **TIPP** (Temperature, Intense exercise, Paced breathing, Paired muscle relaxation), **Radical Acceptance**, **Distraction (ACCEPTS)**.

**Source:** DBT Assignment Workbook; archive.org Linehan collection.
**Use:** One-page “TIPP card” for your workbook.

## Emotion Regulation

**Definition:** DBT module for understanding and changing intense emotions. Steps: **Identify & label emotions → Check the facts → Opposite action → PLEASE** (treat **P**hysical illness, balanced **L**eating, avoid mood-altering substances, balanced **S**leep, **E**xercise).

**Source:** DBT Assignment Workbook + PMC4845754 (attachment ↔ emotion regulation).
**Use:** Pair with behavioural activation tracker.

## Interpersonal Effectiveness

**Definition:** DBT module for asking for what you want and saying no while keeping relationships and self-respect. Core: **DEAR MAN** (Describe, Express, Assert, Reinforce + Mindful, Appear confident, Negotiate), **FAST**, **GIVE**.

**Source:** DBT handouts.
**Use:** Handout + role-play script.

## Mindfulness

**Definition:** Paying attention on purpose, in the present, non-judgmentally. In DBT: **Wise Mind**, **Observe/Describe/Participate**, **One-mindfully**.

**Source:** DBT handouts + Flinders BA.
**Use:** 1-page “Wise Mind” exercise.

## Radical Acceptance

**Definition:** Fully accepting reality as it is, without fighting it, to reduce suffering. Not approval. Practice: “This is the moment; I can’t change the past, but I can cope with the present.”

**Source:** DBT distress tolerance handouts.
**Use:** Half-page reflection prompt.

## Opposite Action

**Definition:** Acting opposite to an emotion’s urge when the emotion doesn’t fit the facts (e.g., approach when fear urges avoidance, if safe).

**Source:** DBT emotion regulation.
**Use:** Worksheet: Emotion → Urge → Opposite action → Outcome.

## PLEASE & TIPP

**PLEASE:** DBT acronym for reducing vulnerability (Physical, Lethargy, Eating, Altering substances, Sleep, Exercise).
**TIPP:** Distress tolerance physiology reset.

**Source:** DBT handouts.
**Use:** Laminated pocket cards.

## Life Story Plan (LS Plan)

**Definition:** DBS Alliance structure for mapping life events into a coherent narrative: Early years → First episode → Supports → Strengths → Turning points → Plan.

**Source:** `https://www.dbsalliance.org/.../LS_plan_workbook.pdf` (863KB, `00 Inbox/a349f530_...pdf`).
**Use:** Your workbook’s narrative/timeline chapter — tie-in to the loneliness theme.

## Coping Skills (general)

**Definition:** Any thought or behavior that helps tolerate or reduce stress. In your scraped set, “coping skills” appears as a transdiagnostic bucket covering BA, DBT, and TF-CBT tools.

**Use:** Glossary umbrella term.
